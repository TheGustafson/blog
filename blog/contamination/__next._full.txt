3d:["$","h2",null,{"children":"The arms race"}]
3e:["$","p",null,{"children":"The pattern is pretty predictable at this point. A new benchmark gets released, the community adopts it as the standard measure for some capability, models improve, scores saturate, someone discovers the benchmark is contaminated, and the community moves on to a new benchmark that then gets contaminated in turn."}]
3f:["$","p",null,{"children":"The cycle has been accelerating. In 2017–2019, a benchmark might remain reliably uncontaminated for a few years because pretraining corpora were smaller and crawled less frequently. By 2022–2024, the lag between benchmark release and contamination was often under a year. By 2025, frontier labs are crawling the web constantly, and any benchmark published in plain text is essentially in a race against the next training run."}]
40:["$","p",null,{"children":"Several attempts at structural fixes have been proposed and partially implemented."}]
41:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"Private test sets."}]," Keep the test questions secret. Don't publish them. GPQA Diamond (graduate-level expert science questions) has moved toward this model, with careful controls over who can access the test questions and under what conditions. 42:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"Dynamic benchmarks."}]," Instead of a static test set, continuously generate new questions. 43:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"Contamination-aware evaluation protocols."}]," Rather than trying to prevent contamination, measure and correct for it. 53:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"High scores on contaminated benchmarks mean nothing.\""}]," This overstates it in the other direction. 58:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"GPQA Diamond."}]," GPQA is a benchmark of expert-level science questions written by PhD-level domain experts, designed specifically to resist contamination.
