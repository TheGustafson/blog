4a:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"MCP and A2A are competitors.\""}]," They aren't. They sit at different layers of the stack. 4b:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"Standards always converge to one winner.\""}]," Sometimes (USB, HTTP, JSON). It fails when the problem splits across layers or when the economic incentives of big players diverge. The agent stack has both. I expect MCP to dominate the model-to-tool layer, A2A or an A2A-descendant to dominate the agent-coordination layer, OpenAPI to remain the canonical schema-for-REST-APIs, and for a bunch of smaller protocols (authentication, identity, billing) to settle independently.
