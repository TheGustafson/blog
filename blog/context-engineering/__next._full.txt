5d:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"More context is always better.\""}]," This is the default assumption and it's wrong in practice. 5e:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"Compaction is the model's job.\""}]," It's not. The model doesn't compact anything. The model produces a response.
