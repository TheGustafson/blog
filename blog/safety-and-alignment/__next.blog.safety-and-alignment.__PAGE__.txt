38:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"RLHF makes models safe.\""}]," It makes them follow instructions in a way labelers endorsed. 39:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"Jailbreaks are solved.\""}]," They aren't. They're managed. 3a:["$","p",null,{"children":[["$","strong",null,{"children":"\"Alignment just means following instructions.\""}]," This one is load-bearing.
