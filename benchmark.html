Crosshair-Bench: Evaluating Frontier Models on an Unseen Game

Wouldn't it be nice to have a board game to evaluate models against that isn't in the training data? Well, I introduce to you Crosshair, a 7×7 connection-and-flip game I invented on October 25, 2026. Should be safe for a few generations yet!

Each move restricts your opponent to the same row or column and 2×2 crosscuts flip deadlocks, so there are no draws, games finish in about 27 moves, and the branching factor stays small (~8 to 12 legal moves per turn). I also trained a 114K-parameter AlphaZero and MCTS engine in Rust (ai-crosshair) to set a baseline for engine strength (random-uct-50, zero-search az-puct-1 anchored at 1200 Elo, az-puct-400, and az-puct-4000). The crosshair-bench harness runs 4-game matches from the ASCII board with colors swapped and scores every move against az-puct-4000 (AZ-4000 Match).
